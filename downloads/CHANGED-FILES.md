## Supabase tables

Both pages sync to their own table, keyed the same way — `profile_id` is the
signed-in user's id and `(profile_id, date)` is unique.

| Page | Table | Migration |
|---|---|---|
| `/cycle` | `cycle_entries` | `supabase/migrations/20260829_cycle_intelligence.sql` |
| `/trackers` | `tracker_days` | `supabase/migrations/20260830120000_tracker_days.sql` |

Until a table exists its page works entirely from the device and says so.
